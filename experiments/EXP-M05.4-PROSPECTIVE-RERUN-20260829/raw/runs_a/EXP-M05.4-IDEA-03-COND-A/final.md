# Baseline de Refinamento de Ideia — EXP-M05.4-IDEA-03-COND-A

## Ideia Original
> Um sistema para conectar leitores de livros raros: ou através de um mapa geográfico de proximidade física entre vizinhos, ou através de um feed assíncrono baseado em afinidade de temas obscuros.

## Resumo do Modelo
A platform that connects collectors and readers of rare books through two discovery modes—a geo‑based map of nearby enthusiasts and an asynchronous feed that matches users by obscure thematic affinities—enabling both local meet‑ups and remote sharing of niche titles.

## Versão Refinada
Develop a web/app platform where users create profiles listing owned and wanted rare books. The system offers two discovery channels: (1) an opt‑in "Nearby Readers" map that shows vetted users within a configurable radius for in‑person meet‑ups, and (2) a "Thematic Feed" powered by AI‑driven tagging of obscure topics, surfacing users with similar interests regardless of location. Features include private messaging, event scheduling, community‑moderated verification badges, optional anonymity, integration with library/catalog APIs, and gamified rewards for sharing and reviewing rare titles.

## Pontos Fortes e Fracos
- **Fortes:** Creates a focused community around rare books, Combines physical proximity and interest‑based discovery, Encourages knowledge exchange and book swapping, Leverages familiar map and feed UI patterns, Potential for gamified incentives and events
- **Fracos:** Niche audience may limit initial user base, Privacy concerns with location sharing, Difficulty verifying authenticity of rare titles, Complexity of building accurate thematic tagging for obscure subjects, Risk of low engagement without strong network effects

## Próximos Passos
Conduct market research and define target collector personas, Design wireframes for map and feed interfaces, Prototype AI tagging pipeline for obscure book themes, Create privacy‑by‑design location sharing controls, Build a minimal viable product with core profile and discovery features, Run a closed beta with a small group of rare‑book collectors, Collect feedback, measure engagement, and iterate on verification and recommendation algorithms
