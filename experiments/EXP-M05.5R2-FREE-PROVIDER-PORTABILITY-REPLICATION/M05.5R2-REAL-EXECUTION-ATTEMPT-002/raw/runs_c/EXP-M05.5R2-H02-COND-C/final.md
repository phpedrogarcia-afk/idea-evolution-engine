## 1. Fonte Humana Imutável (SourceAnchor)

> Tenho pensado que prédios residenciais poderiam ter um espaço pequeno para troca de habilidades entre vizinhos: alguém ajuda com plantas, outro com consertos simples, outro com idiomas. Ainda não sei se isso seria um mural, encontros presenciais ou alguma outra coisa.


## 2. Intenção & Problema Estruturado (Lean First Pass)

- **Intenção do Usuário:** Create a small shared space or system that enables neighbors to trade skills such as gardening, simple repairs, or language tutoring.
- **Problema Interpretado:** Residents lack a structured way to exchange skills and help each other within residential buildings.

## 3. Mecanismo Primário Proposto

**Mecanismo:** Install a community skill‑exchange board (physical or digital) in a common area where residents can post offers and requests.
- **Base de Autoridade Auditada:** `MODEL_HYPOTHESIS`
- **Justificativa:** A centralized board lowers coordination friction and makes skill exchange visible, encouraging participation.


## 4. Alternativas Concorrentes Identificadas

1. **Organize periodic in‑person skill‑swap meetups coordinated by residents.** (Base: `MODEL_HYPOTHESIS`)
   - *Tradeoffs:* Scheduling constraints for participants, Requires a facilitator each session, Limited to attendees present at the time
2. **Develop a building‑specific mobile app for posting and matching skill offers.** (Base: `MODEL_HYPOTHESIS`)
   - *Tradeoffs:* Development and maintenance costs, Potential privacy concerns with user data, Requires residents to have compatible devices
