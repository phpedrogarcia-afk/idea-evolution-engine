## 1. Fonte Humana Imutável (SourceAnchor)

> Um serviço de mentoria reversa para líderes de empresas tradicionais aprenderem sobre novas tecnologias e IA.


## 2. Intenção & Problema Estruturado (Lean First Pass)

- **Intenção do Usuário:** Create a reverse‑mentorship service that pairs senior executives with junior technologists so leaders can learn about new tech and AI directly from those on the front lines.
- **Problema Interpretado:** Traditional corporate leaders often lack up‑to‑date knowledge of emerging technologies and artificial intelligence, creating a gap that hinders strategic adoption and competitive advantage.

## 3. Mecanismo Primário Proposto

**Mecanismo:** Pair each senior leader with a junior employee (or external expert) who acts as a mentor on a scheduled basis (e.g., weekly 1‑hour sessions) to discuss recent developments, use‑cases, and strategic implications of emerging technologies and AI.
- **Base de Autoridade Auditada:** `MODEL_HYPOTHESIS`
- **Justificativa:** The idea explicitly describes a "serviço de mentoria reversa" where leaders learn from junior experts.


## 4. Alternativas Concorrentes Identificadas

1. **Traditional executive training programs (workshops, seminars, MOOCs) delivered by external vendors.** (Base: `MODEL_HYPOTHESIS`)
   - *Tradeoffs:* Higher cost, less personalization, Less interactive
2. **Hiring external consultants to advise leaders on technology strategy.** (Base: `MODEL_HYPOTHESIS`)
   - *Tradeoffs:* Expensive, may lack internal knowledge transfer
3. **Internal innovation labs where leaders observe pilot projects.** (Base: `MODEL_HYPOTHESIS`)
   - *Tradeoffs:* Leaders may not get focused, one‑on‑one guidance
